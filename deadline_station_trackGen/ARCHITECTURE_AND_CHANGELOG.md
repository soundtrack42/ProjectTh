## 1. Three-Stage Architectural Evolution (Baseline from `Report.pdf`)

### Stage 1: Hybrid RL-IPID-MPC & Emulated-Trajectory Shielding
- **Intention:** Bridge Automatic Train Protection (ATP, strict safety bounds) and Automatic Train Operation (ATO, smooth ride & accurate docking) on pre-computed speed profiles.
- **Where & How Implemented:**
  - [`NonlinearTrainPhysics`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/havaei_generalizing_rl.py#L141-L178): Models Davis basic resistance $F_d(v) = A v^2 + B v + C$ ($A=14.5, B=185.0, C=1450\text{ N}$), uphill/downhill grade resistance $F_{\text{grade}}(x) = M g \sin\theta(x)$, curve resistance $F_{\text{curve}}(x)$, and motor constant-power field weakening ($14.0 / v$ above $14.0\text{ m/s}$).
  - [`PureLiuMPC`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/pure_mpc_controller.py#L19-L279): State-augmented LTV-MPC embedding the first-order physical actuator lag $u_{\text{act}}(k) = 0.72\,u_{\text{cmd}}(k) + 0.28\,u_{\text{act}}(k-1)$ directly into the lifted state $z_k = [v_k, u_{\text{act}}(k-1)]^\top$ with proximal horizon weights ($0.65^i$) and a causal plateau guard.
  - [`LearnedLinearizationModel`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/havaei_generalizing_rl.py#L183-L250): Two-head Heteroscedastic Neural Physics Emulator predicting velocity residual $\Delta \hat{v}_\mu(s_k, u_k)$ and state/action log-variance $\log \sigma_\theta^2(s_k, u_k)$ to form the 99% Gaussian Scissor Band $\hat{v}_{\text{emu}}(k) \pm 2.576\,\sigma_{\text{tube}}(k)$.

### Stage 2: Disturbance-Driven Standalone MPC Shielding
- **Intention:** Since $0.28^4 \approx 0.6\%$, reduce MPC horizon to $N_p = 5, N_c = 3$ (cutting QP solve time to $\sim 40\text{--}80\ \mu\text{s}$) and reject unmodeled external storm gusts via an online disturbance observer (DOB) paired with a Scissor Shield.
- **Where & How Implemented:**
  - [`SafetyFilterShield`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/safety_shield.py) & [`PureLiuMPC.select_action`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/pure_mpc_controller.py#L205-L279): Injects the estimated external disturbance acceleration into the affine LTV-MPC offset $r_k \leftarrow r_k + \Delta t \cdot d_{\text{hat}}$.

## 2. Deep-Dive Inspection of `Interesting.pdf` (Bilbao-Moreno et al., 2025) & How It Relates to Our System

**Paper:** Daniel Bilbao-Moreno, Iker Elorza, Eloy Irigoyen, Ignacio Trojaola (2025), *"ATO system for CBTC-based railways: Planning and disturbance estimation for enhanced automation"*, *Results in Engineering* 26, 105357.

### 2.1 Architectural Parallels (Macro / Meso / Micro Hierarchy)
Bilbao-Moreno et al. structure the CBTC-ATO system into three layers (Figs. 1–3, pp. 3–5) that map directly onto our Stage 3 architecture:
1. **Macroscopic Layer (ATS/ATP Network Supervision):** Specifies target station position, operation time (deadline $T_{\text{deadline}}$), ATP speed limits $v_{\lim}(x)$, and receives updated disturbance maps.
   - *In our code:* [`RailroadCorridor`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/deadline_station_trackGen/railroad_corridor.py#L34-L70) and [`generate_multi_station_scenario`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/deadline_station_trackGen/railroad_corridor.py#L233-L393).
2. **Mesoscopic Layer (RMBTP — Reduced-Model Based Trajectory Planning):** Plans optimal speed/position/traction setpoints to meet the target operation time in either **ECO mode** (minimizing energy by lowering peak cruise speed to exactly meet the deadline) or **TURBO mode** (maximizing speed at $90\%$ of ATP limits and arriving early).
   - *In our code:* Our **15-Feature Multi-Property PPO Pilot + Cosine Trajectory Shaper + Contract Shield** operates as an online, closed-loop Mesoscopic Layer, whereas our `1. Max-Speed Greedy Cosine + MPC` baseline corresponds to their **TURBO mode** (with synchronized station landing) and our `4–7. PPO / Contract-Shielded PPO` corresponds to an adaptive **ECO + Punctuality mode**.
3. **Microscopic Layer (Low-Level Powertrain & Tracking Control):** Tracks the mesoscopic setpoints and generates traction/braking commands under actuator saturation.
   - *In our code:* [`PureLiuMPC`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/pure_mpc_controller.py#L19-L279) acting on [`NonlinearTrainPhysics`](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/havaei_generalizing_rl.py#L141-L178).

| **4. | **6. ### Change #3 — Fair Physical Traction & Electrodynamic Regenerative Energy Measurement
- **Problem Diagnosed:**
  - Previously, traction energy was computed as ideal shaft work $F_{\text{prop}} \bar{v} \Delta t$ (ignoring inverter/motor stator-rotor copper losses $R_s |i_s|^2 + R_r |i_r|^2$, current-slewing transient losses, and high-speed field-weakening losses), and braking received a flat $35\%$ regeneration credit even during hard service braking where pneumatic friction brakes dissipate heat.
- **Intention:** Implement a physically fair energy measurement applied identically to all 7 competing methods.
- **Where & How Implemented:**
  - [`dynamic_railroad_mpc.py` (lines 382–403)](file:///home/giorge/Desktop/ProjectTh/deadline_station_trackGen/dynamic_railroad_mpc.py#L382-L403):
